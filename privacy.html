Privacy policy

This website

getshipready.github.io is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use analytics, cookies, trackers or forms. GitHub, as the host, processes visitors' IP addresses to serve the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The free tools

Lite and the quick scan run on your own machine. The quick scan makes no network calls and sends us nothing.

When you buy

Payment is handled by our payment provider, named on the checkout page. We receive your name, email, country and order details from it to deliver your purchase and answer support requests. We never see your full card details.

When you email us or order an Audit

We keep emails to answer them. For an Audit, we use the repository access you give us only to run that audit, and we delete our copy of your code within 30 days of delivering the report.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at jp.norfront+shipready@mail.grokbot.com. If you're in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.
